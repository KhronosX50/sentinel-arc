Our Story

FROM THE FRONTLINES TO THE ENTERPRISE

SentinelArc was born in 2019 out of frustration. Our founders — former NSA analysts, military cyber operators, and elite red teamers — watched from the inside as critical infrastructure fell to attacks that should have been stopped at the perimeter.

The problem wasn't technology. It was strategy. Most security vendors sell tools and dashboards. SentinelArc sells outcomes. We embed with your organization, understand your crown jewels, model your specific threat landscape, and build a defensive posture that holds.

In six years we've grown from a 4-person boutique to a 120-analyst operation spanning three continents, protecting financial institutions, healthcare networks, defense contractors, and critical energy infrastructure across 34 countries.

Our philosophy is simple: the best defense is a relentless offense. We think like the adversary, because most of us were the adversary — in state-sponsored programs, elite military units, and private sector red teams that tested the world's most hardened systems.
